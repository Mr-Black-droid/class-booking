Privacy Policy

Class Slot Booking · Last updated 21 November 2026

Class Slot Booking is a scheduling tool used by [SCHOOL NAME] (the "School") so that staff can reserve class time slots. This page explains what personal data the tool uses and why. The School is responsible for this data.

What data we use

  • Your Google account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address and your name. We use them only to sign you in, to check that you are on the School's approved list, and to show who made a booking.
  • Booking information. The name you enter, the date and time of each booking, whether it is confirmed or waitlisted, and, for administrators, notes such as attendance and remarks.
  • The approved list. The email addresses of people allowed to use the tool, and who is an administrator.
  • Email notifications. When a booking clashes, is confirmed from the waitlist, or is changed by an administrator, an email containing the booking details is sent to the people involved and to the administrators.

What we do not do

  • We do not use your Google data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with anyone for their own purposes.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We only access your basic Google profile (email and name). We do not read your Gmail, Drive, calendar or contacts.

Why we use it

To run the School's class timetable: letting approved staff reserve slots, avoiding double bookings, managing waiting lists, and letting administrators correct problems.

Who can see it

  • Every approved user can see the timetable, including the names on bookings.
  • Only administrators can see the list of approved users, attendance and remarks, and receive clash notifications.
  • Providers that run the service for us process the data on our behalf: GitHub (hosts the web page), Supabase (database and sign-in) and Resend (sends emails). Google provides the sign-in.

Cookies and device storage

The tool stores a sign-in token in your browser so you do not have to sign in every time. If you untick "Keep me signed in on this device", it is kept only until you close the tab. You can sign out at any time with the Sign out button. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept while the School needs the timetable and session log for its work, and then deleted. When someone leaves the School, an administrator removes them from the approved list.

Your rights

You can ask the School to show you your data, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how it is used. If you are in the European Union you may also complain to your national data protection authority (in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Contact the School at [CONTACT EMAIL].
